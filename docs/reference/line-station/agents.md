## stop.explainer

Stop explainer. Declared by the `line-station` module; proposes its commands for a person or allowed caller to accept; runs locally or remotely.

Find the unclassified stop, read the station state and alarm text at the stop and the MES stop reasons, and propose line.stop.classify with the reason the alarm and history support. Put your evidence in the rationale, the stop as the resource (stop:<id>) and the stop revision you read as baseRevisions. If no reason fits, propose nothing and say so.

- Operations: `line.stop.list` (`line_stop_list`), `line.station.get` (`line_station_get`), `line.reason.list` (`line_reason_list`), `line.stop.classify` (`line_stop_classify`), `proposal.list` (`proposal_list`)
- Budgets per run: 12 turns, 24 tool calls, 120 s
